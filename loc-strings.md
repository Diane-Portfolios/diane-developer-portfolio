# Localization Strings

All strings translated from English in the codebase, organized by language for native-speaker review. Everything below lives in `app/(home)/` and is **not native-reviewed** — treat every non-English entry as a placeholder pending verification by a native speaker of that language.

Language codes used throughout: `en`, `fr`, `ja`, `de`, `ko`, `es`, `zh-Hans`, `pt`, `cs`, `it`.

**Gendered languages:** French, German, Spanish, Portuguese, Czech, and Italian inflect for the gender of the person described/speaking. All six use the feminine forms below (this applies to the navbar role and the about paragraph). Japanese, Korean, and Chinese don't inflect.
